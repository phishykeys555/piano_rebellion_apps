How it works

Relative pitch means you hear one note you know (the reference), then work out the next note by how far away it is. You don't need perfect pitch for this. You need distance.

Mystery note: the app says "This is C" and plays it, then plays a mystery note. Tap it on the keyboard (or on the note names, in Settings). Levels 7 and 8 change the reference, so you can't just memorize C.

Scale degree: the app plays I – IV – V – I to set the key, then one note. Name its number in the scale: 1 is home (do), 5 is sol. Level 5 is a minor key, counted from its parent major like every numeral in the studio: in A minor (parent C) the cadence is vi – ii – III – vi (III is E major, borrowing G♯ from harmonic minor), and A is 6, B is 7, C is 1.

Interval: two notes, name the distance. m = minor, M = major, P = perfect, TT = tritone (6 half steps), P8 = octave.

Single tries: one question at a time. Replay the reference or the mystery as often as you like, then press Next. Challenge: 5 rounds of 8 questions and a bonus round. Round 1 is the warm-up that sets your targets; each round's points count up from 0. Right answer = 1 point, right within 3 seconds = 2. One replay of each per question, one try, no second chances.
