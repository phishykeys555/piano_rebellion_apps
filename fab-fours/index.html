How to play Fab Fours

Sixteen words are hiding four groups of four. Pick four that share something, then hit Submit.

Watch for red herrings: some words look like they fit two groups. Only one way works. Three right out of four gets you a One away!

You get 4 mistakes. Win or lose, every group is revealed with why it fits.
