    How it works
      
    What it measures. Your note plays on the piano. Sing it on "ah" and hold it: the timer counts only while your pitch stays inside the window (±30¢ on Level 1, down to ±10¢ on Level 4; 100¢ = one half step). A breath or slip shorter than a quarter second doesn't reset it. "Wobble" is how much your pitch shakes (lower is steadier), "Drift" is whether it creeps sharp or sags flat over the hold.
    
      
    The colors. Green = inside the window (the needle sits in the green band). Orange = close (within 50¢). Red = further off.
    
      
    Levels. Hold the goal time inside the window once to pass: 4, 6, 8, then 12 seconds. Each voice type keeps its own passes. "Sing with me" holds don't unlock levels.
    
      
    Headphones are best: the mic can hear the speaker. Without them, keep the volume moderate.
    
      
    Keys. Space Start or Stop · H "Hear it" · Enter on the results: "Next level" · Esc "Back".
