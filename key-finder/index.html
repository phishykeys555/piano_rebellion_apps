How it works

No sheet music. Just the keys. Learn every key on the piano by sight.

Answer with: tap the screen, play a MIDI keyboard (Chrome or Edge on a computer), or in Find the key, play your own piano and let the microphone hear it. On a real piano any octave counts.

Practice: take your time (turn on Second try in Settings to try again after a miss). Challenge: five 30-second rounds and a bonus round. Round 1 is the warm-up that sets your targets. One try per key, no second chances. Last 10 seconds of a round = 2x points.
