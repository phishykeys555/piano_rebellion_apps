    How it works
      
    What it measures. A piano note plays. Sing it back on "ah" and hold it. Once your voice stays within 50¢ of the note for a moment, the app locks on and listens for 2 seconds: your score is the middle (median) of your pitch in that time, in cents (100¢ = one half step), and how much of the 2 seconds stayed inside the level's window (±25¢, or ±10¢ on Level 3). A hit = the score inside the window and at least half the time inside it. Ten notes a round.
    
      
    The colors. Green = inside the window. Orange = close (within 50¢). Red = further off. On the plot, the blue band is the target and your voice is the line.
    
      
    Levels. 8 hits out of 10 passes a level and shows "Next level". Each voice type keeps its own passes. Practice modes (Loop, "Sing with me") don't unlock levels.
    
      
    Headphones are best: the mic can hear the speaker. Without them, keep the volume moderate and sing after the note stops.
    
      
    Keys. Space Start or Stop · H "Hear it" · Enter on the results: "Next level" · Esc "Back".
