How it works

Tap every quarter note, the whole time. The cowbell plays for a bar or two, then goes silent. Keep tapping right through the silence. When the sound comes back, you'll hear if you're still with it.

Levels: the silent stretches get longer. Level 1 is 2 bars of sound, 1 bar of silence. Level 7 gives you a count-in, then 16 bars of nothing.

Slow tempos are harder. More space between beats means more room to drift. If 96 feels easy, try 60.

What you get: every tap is checked against the true beat, even in the silence. You'll see if you rushed (sped up), dragged (slowed down), how steady you were, and where you landed when the beat came back.

Points: 3 for a tap within about 25 ms of the beat, 2 within 50 ms, 1 within 90 ms (a bit wider at slow tempos). Taps in the silence count double. Extra taps and drifting tempo cost points.

Challenge: 5 rounds and a bonus round, and the silence grows every round. Round 1 is the warm-up that sets your targets. The pulse light and your tap marks stay dark in the silence, so it's all you.

Input: tap the pad or press the space bar. Clap or snap: the microphone hears your claps or finger snaps. One note: play one piano key (pick it in Settings); playing it again while it still rings counts too. MIDI: plug in a digital piano and play any key (Chrome or Edge on a computer). With the microphone on speakers, only the silences are graded (the mic hears the app there); with headphones, every beat. Run Calibrate once per input so the app knows your device's delay.

Settings

Calibration

Every phone, laptop and speaker adds a little delay. Calibrate once per input: tap (or play, or clap) along with 8 clicks.

Microphone

On speakers the mic can hear the cowbell, so it only listens in the silence (that's where it counts). With headphones it grades every beat.
