How it works

Name the note. Say it out loud as you tap. Start Challenge runs timed rounds: a 30-second warm-up sets your targets, then beat each round's target to unlock the next.

Keyboard: A–G to answer. In a key with sharps or flats, tap ♯ or ♭ first (or press = / −). Piano keys must be the exact key, right octave.
