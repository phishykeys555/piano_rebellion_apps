How it works

A groove plays the whole time. The app plays a riff (the call, orange lamp). Then it's your turn (blue lamp): play the same riff back over the same groove, starting on beat 1. Then the next call comes right away, like trading licks in a jam.

Every answer is judged: the right notes in the right order (an extra or missed note only costs that note), and how close each note lands to the call's rhythm. The piano roll shows the call as outlines and your notes in color. Red = wrong note (it shows the note you were after).

Single tries: a set of trades with a summary at the end. Challenge: rounds with a points target, like the other studio apps. Round 1 is the warm-up that sets your targets.

Best with a digital piano and a USB cable (MIDI) in Chrome or Edge. Calibrate timing once per device in Settings.
