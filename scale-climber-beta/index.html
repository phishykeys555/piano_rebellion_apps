A key is clean when every note lands within 50¢ and the notes average 30¢ or closer. Miss a key and you get one more try on it. Timing is measured from each click; Bluetooth headphones add delay.
Optional: if notes you know are on time still read early or late (Bluetooth headphones, a slow speaker), calibrate. Your start note plays, then 12 clicks: sing a short "ta" on that note with each click from the 3rd one. The timing grade then takes off your device's delay. Pitch grades never change. Loop and "Sing with me" are practice modes: graded the same way, but they don't unlock levels or set a best. In Loop the key repeats until you press "Stop" or miss it twice. "Sing with me" keeps the piano playing while you sing, so use headphones (the mic can hear the speaker). Changes apply from the next "Start".

    How it works
      
    What it measures. The pattern plays on the piano with a click on every note, then a count-in, then you sing it back, one note per click (or sing along with "Sing along"). Each note is graded on its pitch, in cents (100¢ = one half step), from the middle of the beat, and on its timing: how early or late you landed on it, from the click. A key is clean when every note lands within 50¢ and the notes average 30¢ or closer; then you climb a half step. Miss a key and you get one more try on it.
    
      
    The colors. Green = clean (within 25¢). Orange = close (within 50¢). Red = off, or no clear note heard. On the plot the bands are the notes and your voice is the line (green when on pitch).
    
      
    Levels. 5 clean keys in a row passes a level (or reaching the top of your voice type's range with at least 2) and shows "Next level". Each voice type keeps its own passes. Practice modes (Loop, "Sing with me") don't unlock levels.
    
      
    Headphones are best: the mic can hear the speaker. Bluetooth headphones add delay: use "Calibrate timing" in "Settings".
    
      
    Keys. Space Start or Stop · H "Hear it" · Enter on the results: "Next level" · Esc "Back".
