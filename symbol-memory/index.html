How it works

Classic Memory: every symbol is on two identical cards, the symbol big with its name written under it. Flip two cards. If they match, they stay up and you see what the symbol does. If not, they flip back. Remember where things are.

Learn: press Peek to see every card for 3 seconds before you start. Tap a matched pair any time to read (and hear) its meaning again.

Play: the normal game. Fewest moves wins. Your best for each level and size is saved on this device.

Challenge: five rounds and a bonus round. Points count up from 0 each round toward a target: 100 a match, +50 for every match in a row, plus a speed bonus when you clear the board. Hit the target to unlock the next round. Each round adds pairs or harder symbols.

Keyboard: arrow keys move between cards, Enter or Space flips.
