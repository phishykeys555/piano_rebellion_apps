Settings

Name That Sound clips always play: they are the question.

How to play

Four choices, 20 seconds. Answer fast for up to 1000 points; a correct answer at the buzzer still gets 500.

Get answers right in a row for a multiplier: x1.2 at 2, x1.5 at 3, x2 at 5. The last question of a 10 is worth double.

After every answer you get the why. That is the point.

Modes

  • Daily 10: the same 10 for everyone today, mixed topics, easy to hard. Share your squares.
  • Quick Play: 10 from the topics you tap. None tapped means all.
  • Streak Run: keep going until 3 misses.
  • Party: 2 to 6 players pass one device. Podium at the end.

Lifelines (one each per run)

  • 50/50 removes two wrong answers.
  • Skip swaps the question for another at the same level.
  • Freeze stops the clock for this question.

Progress

Earn XP, climb from Open Mic to Legend, and fill each topic's mastery. 80% mastery earns that topic's crown. Finish a run every day to keep your streak; every 7 days earns a streak freeze that covers one missed day.

Keys

1 to 4 answer, Enter continue, R replay the sound.
